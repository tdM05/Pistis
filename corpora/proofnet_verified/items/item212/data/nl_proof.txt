We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\bar{a} \cdot \bar{b}) \cdot \bar{c} &=\overline{a \cdot b} \cdot \bar{c} \\
&=\overline{(a \cdot b) \cdot c} \\
&=\overline{a \cdot(b \cdot c)} \\
&=\bar{a} \cdot \overline{b \cdot c} \\
&=\bar{a} \cdot(\bar{b} \cdot \bar{c})
\end{aligned}
$$
since integer multiplication is associative.
